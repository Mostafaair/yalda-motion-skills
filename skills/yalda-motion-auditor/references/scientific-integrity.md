# Engineering visual integrity

## Mandatory source distinction
For every visual entity record:
- `provenance`: actual software model/export; analysis output; approved conceptual model; decorative placeholder; or UNKNOWN.
- `quantity`: geometry, load, vertical stress, settlement, subgrade modulus Ks, patch class, spring coefficient or unquantified visual.
- `units`, sign convention, coordinate system, value range, mapping, reference build, exaggeration factor and disclaimer where applicable.

No numerically plausible values may be fabricated from screenshots. Do not infer real stress/settlement/Ks from color alone. Do not conflate load arrows with solved lateral-pressure/stress contributions.

## YALDA invariants
- Foundation strip widths/openings and patch geometry match approved source rather than regenerated by image interpolation.
- N2, N3 and N4 neighbor shadows and sizes derive from approved reference, not arbitrary 2D stretching.
- Patches retain IDs, location and approved color between 3D, plan and SAFE; colors represent consistent classification or quantity.
- A color ramp for continuous stress/settlement should not be reused to imply categorical patch identity without legend or mapping.
- Field appearance under scroll is **narrative revelation**, not necessarily time-dependent stress propagation. Stop scrolling ≠ indefinite growing settlement.
- If deformation visually exaggerated, shape/direction and amount are explicitly labeled conceptual; avoid fictitious engineering numbers in UI.

## Methods of review
1. Lock reference model and exported patch map if provided.
2. Overlay key geometry landmarks at equal camera/viewport states.
3. Inspect displacement and camera separately; record angular/projection discrepancy.
4. Verify scientific field against its declared source: not all contour fields imply a solver. For actual numerical claims require engineering calculation/verification separately from visual audit.
5. For SAFE destination, verify semantic and geometric mapping; avoid claiming actual SAFE readback from a promotional animation.

References are not engineering-code certifications. Source reports may cite USACE for stress superposition; the specific figures and current edition must be inspected independently before asserting compliance. Historic report only referred to Chapter 6, figs 6-4 to 6-6.
